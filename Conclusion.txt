This project demonstrates customer segmentation using K-Means Clustering and Principal Component 
Analysis (PCA). After preprocessing the data by removing unnecessary columns, encoding categorical 
values, and standardizing the numerical features, the Elbow Method was used to determine the optimal 
number of clusters. The K-Means algorithm successfully grouped customers with similar purchasing 
behaviour, while PCA reduced the high-dimensional dataset to two principal components for effective 
visualization. Customer segmentation enables businesses to identify different customer groups and 
design targeted marketing strategies, personalized promotions, and loyalty programs. One limitation of 
K-Means clustering is that the number of clusters must be specified in advance, and the algorithm is 
sensitive to outliers. An important advantage of PCA is that it simplifies complex datasets while 
preserving most of the useful information for visualization and analysis.
